What is mail merge?

Mail merge joins one template with a list of people to make a personal copy for everyone — 200 rows in, 200 finished certificates or emails out.

📄 Template+📋 List→✉️ One copy each

This app does the making. Power Automate (in your Microsoft 365) does the sending from your own mailbox.

Email text

Just write the message. “Dear <participant name>,” is added at the top for you. Coloured chips like Event name are filled in per email — click a button to add one; they can't be broken by accident.

Send with Power Automate

1Generate here — you get a ZIP
2Extract it into your OneDrive - Hexaware folder (File Explorer)
3Click Run on your flow — everyone gets their own email with all their files

One-time setup · about 3 minutes

  1. Check OneDrive syncs on your laptop: open File Explorer — you should see OneDrive - Hexaware in the left sidebar. (If not, open the OneDrive app from the Start menu and sign in with your Hexaware account.)
  2. ⬇ Download the flow (v2) — keep it zipped
  3. make.powerautomate.com ▸ My flows. If you imported the old Mail Merge - Send, delete it (⋯ ▸ Delete).
  4. Import ▸ Import Package (Legacy) ▸ Upload the zip.
  5. Under Related resources click Select during import for OneDrive for Business and for Office 365 Outlook ▸ pick your account ▸ Save. Then Import.
  6. Open the flow ▸ Turn on.
  7. Test: tick 🧪 Test mode, generate certificates for 2–3 names, then follow “Every event” below. You should get 3 [TEST] emails.

Every event · about 1 minute

  1. Untick 🧪 Test mode. Generate certificates (or the voucher mail kit).
  2. In File Explorer ▸ OneDrive - Hexaware: delete the old Mail Merge folder.
  3. Right-click the ZIP in Downloads ▸ Extract All ▸ Browse ▸ pick OneDrive - Hexaware ▸ Extract. Wait for the green ✓ on the new Mail Merge folder (synced).
  4. Power Automate ▸ My flows ▸ Mail Merge - Send ▸ Run ▸ Run flow.

Works for any number of people — big lists are split into parts automatically and sent one after another (about 2–3 seconds per email, so 300 people ≈ 15 minutes; you can close the page, it keeps going).

Something went wrong?
  • “Read the mail list” — not found: the Mail Merge folder must sit directly inside OneDrive - Hexaware, not inside another folder. Wait for the green ✓ before running.
  • Old people got emailed: the old Mail Merge folder was still there — always delete it before extracting.
  • Email too big: each email carries all formats you ticked. Untick PNG (it's the largest) if attachments get heavy.
  • A few emails failed: open the run, click the red step to see why (usually a mistyped email). The others still go out.
  • Sending from a team mailbox: in the flow replace Send the email with Send an email from a shared mailbox (V2) (needs Send-As rights).
